Included: beta samples D, D Darkness, E (Vivi), F and G; female and male

Original files are unmodified. See SOURCES.json for download hashes, archive
members and individual CC0 notices for the eight additions.

Drop additional self-contained .vrm files (at most 25,000,000 bytes) here,
then rescan in ASSETS. Network downloads persist under SHA-256 filenames.
