DE and ST base-game expansions

DE maps and texture art retain their original authorship. Source downloads and exact texture donor hashes are documented in sources.json and texture-sources.json. Original archive notices are retained in the de-* subdirectories.

ST Classic maps adapt Tribes 2 mission/terrain/interior layouts from the pinned t2-mapper repository. Native ST vehicle geometry and bronze/gunmetal atlas are independently authored. Source mission provenance is recorded in st-sources.json.
