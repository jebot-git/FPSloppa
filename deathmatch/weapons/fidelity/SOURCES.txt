2026-10-01 furniture additions: Britdawgmasterfunk's CC0 Firearms_kit_1.0
stock.009 supplies the five revised ST shoulder stocks; grip.001 supplies
Glock/USP/Deagle hand furniture. Disc cover, curved laser fore-end, rounded
pistol guards, receiver mounts and closed mortar breech are authored edits.
Krita furniture-surfaces.kra contains the revised photographic finish layers.

Final topology optimization: original baked UVs and vertex normals preserved
with meshoptimizer v1.0 (MIT) as an offline authoring tool. No library is linked
into the game. Laser/rotary ST barrels use octagonal walls; mortar uses 12 sides.
Per-model before/after counts are recorded in sources.json optimization entries.

Shared shoulder stock follow-up: all five stock.009 instances reduced to 960
triangles each in editable Blender sources and runtime scenes. Existing atlas,
materials and node transforms retained; all non-stock mesh arrays unchanged.
Reports and visual comparisons: test-results/weapon-stock/.

Shared donor optimization follow-up: grip.006 / ContouredLowerReceiver is now
1,400 triangles on all ten ST instances; body.001 / InstrumentReceiverShell
is 2,400 on all four. Both miniguns' front and mid stabilizers are 1,400 each;
Glock/USP/Deagle SculptedPistolGrip is 1,200 each. Both complete railguns are
9,400 triangles. Total saving: 54,930 triangles across 17 weapons.
Editable blends, interchange GLBs, runtime scenes and build budgets agree.
Authored split normals are reprojected per face after decimation to preserve
hard edges. Existing texture atlases/materials and all non-target mesh arrays,
node transforms and gameplay metadata are retained. No new donor assets.
Counts, shape deviation samples and before/after views:
test-results/weapon-shared-reduction/.
