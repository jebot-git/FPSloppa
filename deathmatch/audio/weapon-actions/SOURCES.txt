FPSloppa weapon action / lifecycle sound bank, 2026-10-01

These are authored interpretations of weapon behavior, not commercial-game samples.
Existing main reports and embedded shotgun actions remain in their current banks.
All new cues are mono 32 kHz PCM with prewarmed compressed .res runtime resources.
No gameplay firing delay, ammunition use or damage was changed.

Authoring
1. tools/edit_weapon_action_stems.py: installed Audacity MCP edits six source
   stems with band limiting, DC removal and -6 dBFS normalization. Other
   Audacity projects are preserved. Edited WAVs are retained in
   tools/audio-sources/weapon-actions/edited/; MCP call log is in
   test-results/weapon-actions-20261001/audacity-edits.json.
2. tools/build_weapon_actions.py: layer, trim and pitch recorded machinery,
   liquid and air; master to role-specific 50 ms RMS targets (-21 to -30 dBFS),
   with -6 dBFS sample ceiling. Loops use overlap crossfades, no silent gaps.
3. Godot --headless --xr-mode off --path . --script tools/import_weapon_actions.gd
   preserves loop metadata in exported resources.
4. tools/review_weapon_actions.py creates a 50-second audition and cue timeline.

Runtime
profiles.gd declares select, charge, firing loop, recovery and stop roles.
player.gd follows accepted/predicted feedback once, charge snapshots and the
current held weapon. Eight reusable nearby positional loop voices use Effects
volume, distance attenuation and wall muffling. Delayed cues share the existing
32-voice one-shot mixer. Switching/death cancels pending actions; loops fade
before reassignment and clear on menus, map changes, backend reset/disconnect.
BFG startup and Doom/Quake/CS shotgun action already exist in the report bank;
new duplicate cues are not added. CS physical AWP cycling keeps its real hand
interaction sounds; the new automatic bolt tail is desktop-only.
