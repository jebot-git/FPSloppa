# Scout cloaking device

Adapted from **Energy Pipe** by **musdasch**, licensed **CC0 1.0**.
Source: https://opengameart.org/content/energy-pipe
Archive: https://opengameart.org/sites/default/files/EnergyCell.zip

The reproducible Blender conversion is `tools/cloak_device/export.py`. It removes the shine billboard and hidden inner cylinder, reduces coplanar/radial geometry, preserves source UVs, reduces the texture to 256×256, and sizes the cell to 18 cm tall. The opaque cyan energy shell avoids layered transparent rendering. Source archive and authoring file remain under tools/cloak_device/source; packages contain the runtime GLB/texture/manifest only.
