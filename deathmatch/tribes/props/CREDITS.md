# ST field equipment artwork

The station, turret, sensor, generator and beacon designs are independently
modeled adaptations, studied from the [original Starsiege Tribes manual,
printed pages 46–51](https://www.the-flet.com/dynamix/t1/TribesManual.pdf) and
[original-game screenshots](https://www.old-games.ru/game/screenshots/733.html).
No retail geometry or texture pixels are included.

Mechanical cores are modified from **Kenney Space Kit 2.0**, licensed CC0:
[asset page](https://kenney.nl/assets/space-kit). Retained sources and the license
are in `tools/tribes/prop-sources`: `turret_single`, `turret_double`,
`machine_generator`, `machine_generatorLarge`, `desk_computerScreen`, and
`satelliteDish_detailed`. They were resized/refinished and incorporated into
new outer housings, split station pylons, turret yokes, emitters and sensor units.

The original bronze/gunmetal vector atlas is shared with the vehicle redesign:
`deathmatch/vehicles/tribes/hull-atlas.svg`, generated by
`tools/tribes/vehicle_texture.py`. Original matching SVG purchase icons are
reproducible with `tools/tribes/prop_icons.py`.

`tools/tribes/prop_models.py` rebuilds the 22 equipment models and Stonehenge
station-backing cladding in Blender MCP. `tools/tribes/prop-sources/st-equipment.blend`
is the editable source. `tools/tribes/import_props.gd` exports native compressed
Godot scenes with one shared mipmapped texture resource. Runtime colours and
power status are applied by `deathmatch/tribes/prop_library.gd`.
