# Tribes vehicle artwork

The Scout, LPC and HPC retain a resized keel from **craft_speederA** in [Space Kit 2.0 by Kenney](https://kenney.nl/assets/space-kit), licensed CC0. The original license is retained in `tools/tribes/vehicle-sources/License.txt`.

The outer hulls, wings, fins, engine pods, recessed passenger bays and controls were independently remodeled in Blender MCP with the original Starsiege Tribes silhouettes as visual references. The bronze/gunmetal panel atlas and purchase icons are original SVG artwork. No retail geometry or texture pixels are shipped.

Visual references: [original manual, printed pages 48–49](https://www.the-flet.com/dynamix/t1/TribesManual.pdf), [original-game screenshot gallery](https://www.old-games.ru/game/screenshots/733.html), and the Tribes Wiki's [Scout](https://tribes.fandom.com/wiki/Scout), [LPC](https://tribes.fandom.com/wiki/Light_Personnel_Carrier), and [HPC](https://tribes.fandom.com/wiki/Heavy_Personnel_Carrier) illustrations. Reference downloads are retained only as ignored validation evidence; these are independent approximations with cockpit/seat geometry adapted to the working game mounts.

`tools/tribes/vehicle_models.py` reproduces all three models; `scout_model.py` and `transport_models.py` select subsets of that same builder. `tools/tribes/vehicle-sources/vehicles.blend` is the current editable Blender source. `vehicle_texture.py` generates the editable atlas SVG and `vehicle_texture.gd` rasterizes it. Native compressed Godot scenes include mipmapped textures; all static parts are batched into five material surfaces plus two separate cockpit grips.

The turbine loop is original synthesized audio generated by `tools/tribes/scout_audio.py`; it contains no third-party recordings.

Wildcat, Shrike, Havoc, Beowulf, Thundersword and Jericho are independently constructed native Godot meshes from
`tools/t2_vehicles/models.gd`. They use the same original bronze/gunmetal atlas,
face-aligned panel, hatch and vent UVs, mipmapped anisotropic textures, neutral
team panels and blue engine emission as the existing ST fleet. Runtime team
coloring remains controlled by the `TeamPanel` material name. These models do
not include retail Tribes 2 geometry or texture pixels.
