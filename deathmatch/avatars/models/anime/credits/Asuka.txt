Model description:          This model is based on the Evangelion characters Rei & Asuka.
                            
                            Rei is extremely timid, shy & at times almost seems withdrawn
                            from the world around her. All of Rei's personnel records have
                            been erased, & there is no trail leading to her past.
                            Rei is also a by-product of the human instrumentality project,
                            artificially created & implanted with the soul of Yui Ikari.

                            Asuka is the bright girl of the bunch with fiery red hair & a
                            temper to match. Pilot of the first official Evangelion Unit-02,
                            Asuka is probably the only Eva pilot who actually enjoys & takes
                            pride in being a pilot.
                           
                            This character is my interpretation of Asuka in a heavly
                            modified costume. The intention was to use one model and make
                            many skins of different anime characters in one package.

Installation instructions:

To use this model you need the Advanced Model Support mod & UnrealTournament version 436
or later. AMS can be downloaded at www.pseudorandom.co.uk

Extract the "Asuka.umod file from the zip file, & run it by double-clicking it.
All files should be automatically placed in their proper UT directories.
Select Team Anime from the player setup then start a practice session.

For Lan games or Dedicated servers:

Open the unrealtourament.ini in the system directory of UnrealTournament & add these
lines under the [Engine.GameEngine] where you see ServerPackages= Close & save.
The umod file should automatically place the serverpackages in the unrealtourament.ini
Also don't forget that both the server & client must have the model installed.

ServerPackages=Asuka
ServerPackages=AsukaSkins
