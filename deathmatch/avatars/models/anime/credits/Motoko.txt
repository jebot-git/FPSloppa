
Introduction:

Hi, thanks for looking at my first skin, ever!  The character is "Motoko Kusanagi", from the manga artist, Masamune Shirow.

She is the lead character in the "Ghost in the Shell" manga.
(Manga = Japanese Comix).


Installation:

There are included in this zip file, an *.int file and an *.utx file.

Please put the *.int file into the .\UnrealTournament\System folder.

Please put the *.utx file into the .\UnrealTournament\Textures folder.

After that, you can launch UT and goto Player Setup to see if the skin was installed correctly.  Just choose Female Commando and you should see Motoko as an added option.

There is support for Team Colors, (RBGY), and also some extra faces/hair colors to experiment with.  Her hair is sometimes blue in the actual Manga.

Caveats / Known issues:

I use the skins at LAN parties and run it on a server.
I added the "ServerPackages=FCommando_Motoko" to the UnrealTournament.ini and it appears to work fine.  However, the skin does not work correctly with the latest version of UT (v428) but works fine with (v420).  I don't know why, but for some reason, UT thinks the skins are illegal.
