# FPSloppa 0.22v — Final expansion and Assault conversions

This release is being prepared. The final expansion download is staged on the
draft; matching game binaries must be built and validated before publication.

The separate Final Expansion contains 78 new maps: 22 Tribes 2 Classic ST,
14 DE, 31 classic arena, seven ChaosUT KOTH and four UT Assault conversions.
Combined map lists retain the 32 original base maps. Legacy DE maps and the ten
RCMD Q2/Q3/Quetoo adaptations are excluded. DE/ST additions also remain in the
base assets. The expansion selection is closed; future updates are necessary
fixes only.

The four Assault maps are Pumpfac, Skyville, Twintower and Atlantica. Their
ordered objectives use native switches and destructible targets, including
health depletion, locked future objectives and paired attack/defend legs.
The runtime now supports one to eight ordered AS objectives and up to 128 maps
per rotation. Imported low passages use crouch-capable navigation; documented
ramps retain otherwise unsuitable stair routes.

The release also includes the six Tribes 2 vehicle adaptations, ST weather and
ambience, prepared mipmaps and lightmaps, DE material penetration, bot wallbang
awareness, incoming-fire reactions and movement smoothing from the expansion
work. Single authored KOTH hills stay fixed; multiple hills retain timed
rotation.

See the expansion's README and frozen manifest for installation instructions,
source notices, checksums and validation limits. Archive integrity and route
connectivity are not claims of competitive balance or perfect bot play.
