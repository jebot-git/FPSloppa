Thirteen recordings selected for FPSloppa. Adaptations: uniform attenuation of 2.97403 dB and Ogg Vorbis quality 4 encoding, retaining 44.1 kHz stereo. No pitch, timing, or timbre changes. Modified recordings retain CC BY-SA 4.0. These are independent recordings by VoiceBosch, not audio extracted from UT or Quake.

`manifest.json` records exact source URLs and SHA-256 hashes of originals and encoded files. Original WAVs remain available at those URLs. Reproduce each conversion with `ffmpeg -i source.wav -af volume=-2.97403dB -c:a libvorbis -q:a 4 output.ogg` (metadata affects byte hashes).
