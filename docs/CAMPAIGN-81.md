# Vesper campaign: 81 districts

Default CQ layout on `experimental/cq-districts`: **81 districts, 128 admitted players total, 16 reservations per district**. It uses 81 independently compiled BSP29 maps and 21 regional gateways with at most four workers each. Waiting players count toward 128; a transferring player counts once globally while reserving source and destination district slots. Admission is atomic in the coordinator, including across regions and coordinator frontends. Existing campaign databases adopt the cap on upgrade; existing sessions can resume, and an older over-cap database rejects new players until it falls below 128. This is an admission policy, not a measured 128-player combat performance claim. Ordinary game modes, their server limits and `main` remain unchanged. The live branch now supports CQ/UT99 only; see [deployment, persistence and VPS sizing](CQ-LIVE-DEPLOYMENT.md).

## Campaign rules

- Four homebases and four initially neutral relays take 30 points. The 32 perimeter districts take 10. The 40 outskirts and inner city have no capture objectives.
- The circle is 8 m in radius, with a 2.5 m vertical tolerance. One point per second, independent of the number of teammates. Contested capture pauses; the absent team's progress resets. Capturing resets both point counters. Workers report live, resident identities from actual circle positions; clients cannot submit capture points. Reports expire after one second and replayed reports cannot extend that deadline.
- Defenders can enter their homebase. Opponents must control all eight of its perimeters. Eligibility is checked when reserving and before committing an entry. Losing eligibility aborts an uncommitted transfer and restores its source escrow. A committed transfer is never rolled back.
- Holding a homebase and all eight perimeters continuously for 3,600 seconds starts a 3,600-second perimeter lockdown. Enemy perimeter capture cannot advance during lockdown. A new uninterrupted one-hour holding period starts after lockdown expires. Losing part of the perimeter cancels a holding interval.
- At **00:00 UTC**, each controlled homebase awards its owner one victory point. There is no instant all-base victory. At 20 points, the campaign result is recorded and ownership, capture progress, timers and scores reset. Players return through reinforcement deployment. Pending transfers resolve safely before reset deployment. Simultaneous 20-point finishes at the same midnight are recorded as a draw, avoiding arbitrary base-order bias.
- The scoreboard shows current points and the four latest results. The durable coordinator retains the latest 32 results. Ownership and scheduled awards persist through restart; downtime advances against the last committed ownership, and stale capture reports expire. Daily awards and resets use the same SQLite transaction or etcd CAS as ownership, so multiple frontends cannot award twice.
- The inner city blocks firing and damage on the authoritative worker, including transferred held-fire state. It has no pickups. High-tier homebase equipment follows logical campaign roles, not the legacy 16-slot physics coordinate adapter.
- Death releases the resident slot only through a worker-validated respawn request. Deployment chooses the nearest online, non-full, team-controlled district by physical graph distance, with district ID as a deterministic tie-break. Controlled relays count as allied territory. If **no allied destination has an available slot**, deployment falls back to the nearest online, non-full neutral relay perimeter, city perimeter or inner city. These are the 40 `outskirts` districts and the `hub`; neutral relay capture districts and enemy-owned territory are excluded. Allied territory takes priority even when a neutral district is closer. Draining, offline and fully reserved districts are skipped in both searches. With neither an allied nor a neutral destination available, the client presents an isolated waiting room. Ownership and district availability changes retry waiting deployment automatically. Persistent identities and custom VRMs survive logout; inactive profiles are archived outside the live player budget. Already admitted players can always enter reinforcement waiting; waiting does not grant extra places beyond the global 128-player cap.

## Maps and materials

Every district is a separate local-coordinate 250×250 m map. The full connected plan spans 2.25 km per side, but no BSP or physics simulation contains that whole extent. Layouts have distinct street bends and seeded buildings; the manifest rejects street-plan copies even after rotation or reflection. Enclosed, mixed and open districts combine multilevel halls, ramps, balconies, bridges, alleys, stalls, vehicles, street lamps and small sky courts. Roads remain two-car width (7 m). Enclosed maps retain optional 4 m jetpack gaps with pedestrian alternatives.

Night lighting uses full VIS and RGB bounce baking with dirt, the existing night panorama, and conservative occluders inside solid building mass. Holograms, flags and the scoreboard add no real-time lights. Existing two-effect emissive safeguards are unchanged. Each BSP must remain version 29, below 25 MB, with vertices/faces/marksurfaces below 65,535 and nodes/leaves/clipnodes below 32,767. Preparation validates lightmap overflow, navigation routes, spawn/pickup access, open gateways, covered streets, sky courts and capsule clearance through the optional jetpack gaps.

The expanded Makkon palette uses original industrial walls, masonry, ceilings, containers and copper/bronze/brass/silver/iron/rust/gold metal families. Original pixel data and all four mip levels are retained; native record hashes are checked in the embedded BSPs. Each district has texture provenance, and `maps/CampaignDistricts/materials.json` records the expanded palette's pinned archives. [Makkon permissions and credits](../tools/makkon/README.md) apply; these textures are not used for generative imagery. Existing LibreQuake materials remain part of the city palette. Authored UV density is preserved to bound lightmap size.

## Run the experiment

Validation on 20 September 2026: all 81 maps passed 9,398 navigation checks and 864 gateway rays, with zero invalid/overflowed baked faces. The atlas contains 31 enclosed, 35 mixed and 15 open layouts, 94 native Makkon materials, and a largest BSP of 10,716,192 bytes. The original sixteen rule/transaction tests passed; the 128-player update expands this to 23 tests, including concurrent admission against a persisted database and allied-first neutral deployment. [128-player network admission results](validation/campaign-128-capacity.json) verify 128 accepted and 12 rejected concurrent attempts across three gateways, 64 waiting players counted against the limit, closed full districts, automatic allied/neutral redeployment and logout freeing an admission slot. [81-worker protocol results](validation/cluster-81-network.json), [etcd failover and UTC scoring](validation/campaign-81-ha.json), and [live Godot/Vulkan campaign results](validation/campaign-81-live.json) record the tested scope.

The separate client is a Vulkan desktop prototype. It renders one map at a time and supports authoritative movement, jetpack inputs, firing, terminal/physical transfers, colored placeholder actors, capture flags, the scoreboard and the waiting room. It uses snapshot smoothing, **not the shipping client's prediction, weapon presentation, voice or VR integration**. It cannot enter the normal lobby or normal game modes.

The standard source launchers now select this campaign. Build the console-only worker package once (command below), then run:

```sh
./launch-conquest.sh --server
./launch-conquest.sh
```

The server supervisor creates private configuration under `test-results/cq-campaign`, starts the coordinator, all 21 regional gateways and ENet facades, and all 81 map workers. It retains the durable campaign database and stops its child services on shutdown. This is a local development deployment; independently distributed services remain available below. A smaller local smoke test keeps the full atlas but starts only selected workers:

```sh
./launch-conquest.sh --server --state-dir test-results/cq-small --base-port 48700 --districts d13 d40
./launch-conquest.sh test-results/cq-small/client.json
```

The [two-worker default launch path](validation/campaign-128-launch.json) was verified with the real hub/relay BSP workers and an attached Vulkan desktop client. Other destinations stay closed until their workers are online. `launch-conquest-district-maps.sh` aliases the same default. The older prototype launchers are no longer supported by the CQ-only live package.

To provision services separately, create private configuration and build the console-only worker package:

```sh
python3 -m tools.district_cluster init --config /private/campaign/cluster.json
python3 tools/build_console_server.py --template Builds/ClusterPrototype/FPSloppaServer.x86_64 --output Builds/CQLive --campaign-maps
python3 -m tools.district_cluster coordinator --config /private/campaign/cluster.json --database /private/campaign/control.sqlite
```

Use a fresh coordinator database when switching between generic and campaign profiles. For each assigned gateway, run its backend and ENet facade. Example for the inner city, whose gateway is g10:

```sh
python3 -m tools.district_cluster gateway --config /private/campaign/cluster.json --name g10
python3 -m tools.district_cluster edge-config --config /private/campaign/cluster.json --name g10 --output /private/campaign/g10-edge.json
godot --headless --xr-mode off --path . --script tools/district_cluster/edge_main.gd -- /private/campaign/g10-edge.json
python3 -m tools.district_cluster worker-config --config /private/campaign/cluster.json --district d40 --state-dir /private/campaign/workers --output /private/campaign/d40.json
Builds/CQLive/FPSloppaServer.x86_64 -- --asset-root Builds/CQLive --experimental-cq --cq-district-maps --cq-worker 8 --cluster-worker /private/campaign/d40.json
python3 -m tools.district_cluster client-config --config /private/campaign/cluster.json --district d40 --team 0 --output /private/campaign/client.json
./start-campaign-client.sh /private/campaign/client.json
```

Repeat workers for d00…d80, using each row's `map_slot`; repeat gateways g00…g20. Offline destinations remain closed, so partial local deployment is supported. Backend and ENet addresses default to loopback. Tunnel them for separate hosts; this change does not deploy a remote server. The [cluster guide](PERSISTENT-DISTRICT-CLUSTER.md) covers multiple frontends and etcd. The campaign atlas is fixed at 81 districts; generic profile topology remains dynamically configurable from 4 to 81.

## Rebuild and verify

Use EricW 0.18.1 and the existing `tools/cq_maps/requirements.txt` environment. The approved native texture archives can be restored with `tools/makkon/fetch.py` if absent. `campaign_seeds.json` pins layouts that passed navigation. Geometry compilation recreates the source maps; expanded materials are applied in a second reproducible pass.

```sh
/tmp/cq-map-tools/bin/python tools/cq_maps/campaign.py --compiler /path/to/ericw/bin --jobs 4
/tmp/cq-map-tools/bin/python tools/cq_maps/campaign_materials.py --archives tools/makkon/local --compiler /path/to/ericw/bin --jobs 4
python3 tools/cq_maps/campaign_prepare.py --jobs 2
python3 tools/cq_maps/campaign_manifest.py
/tmp/cq-map-tools/bin/python tools/cq_maps/campaign_plan.py
python3 -m unittest tools.district_cluster.test_model tools.district_cluster.test_campaign tools.district_cluster.test_admission
python3 -m tools.district_cluster.campaign_capacity_test
python3 -m tools.district_cluster.network_test --districts 81
python3 -m tools.district_cluster.ha_test --etcd /path/to/etcd --output /tmp/fresh-campaign-ha
python3 -m tools.district_cluster.campaign_game_test --binary Builds/CQLive/FPSloppaServer.x86_64 --output /tmp/fresh-campaign-game
```

The last test starts and stops its own local hub/relay workers and a Vulkan desktop probe. Use fresh test output directories. The older generic 81-worker test uses synthetic protocol payloads and verifies 1,296 reservations independently of CQ; CQ admission is capped at 128. The generic test does not certify 1,296 concurrent combat clients, WAN performance or 81 fully occupied physics simulations. The worker backend retains the existing limitation that transfer journals are durable but continuous combat state is not checkpointed every tick. SQLite is local persistence; distributed durability requires the etcd deployment.
